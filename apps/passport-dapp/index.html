Create a Passport smart account with a passkey, reopen it after a reload, and connect a wallet seeded from the same passkey. You can also open a known ACC by address with an enrolled passkey. The service proves and sponsors every transaction on the Midnight network this build is pinned to.

Actions

  • Enter the ACC address in “Open by ACC address” on the joining browser first.

  • Needs an open account.

    Prototype limits
    
        
      
          
    • 
            The ACC viewing secret is random. It is sealed to this passkey's
            network-specific reader key in the ACC inbox. Create and open are available here;
            viewing-key rotation needs a verified device and grant roster.
          
      • 
          
    • 
            Mock mode is for development only. With ?mockPasskey under
            the Vite dev server, a software passkey kept in this browser's localStorage stands in
            for your authenticator, and a banner says so. Production builds do not contain it.
          
      • 
          
    • 
            Your passkey provider must support PRF, as Google Password Manager and
            iCloud Keychain do. A passkey without PRF cannot create an account or open the wallet.
