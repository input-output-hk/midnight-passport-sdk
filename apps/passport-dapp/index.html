Midnight Passport — prototype

Not connected.

Creating an account asks for your passkey three times: to create it, to verify the authenticator, and to derive the account's encryption key from the passkey's PRF. Rotating asks once more, and proving on the service takes about 30 seconds.

The account's first encryption key is derived from your passkey (Lace recipe v1, MIP-0015), so it is bound to this network and reproducible. Prototype only: rotating replaces it with a random key that is not retained, so data encrypted to a rotated key is not recoverable.

The built-in wallet is a read-only stand-in for the DApp Connector. Its seed comes from your passkey's PRF output, which needs a second prompt: first choose the passkey, then confirm to derive the seed. A passkey without PRF cannot create an account or open the wallet. It never proves or pays; the service sponsors.
