Midnight Passport — prototype

Not connected.

Creating an account asks for your passkey twice: once to create it, and once to verify the authenticator. Rotating asks once more, and proving on the service takes about 30 seconds.

Prototype only: the encryption keys are random and not retained, so encrypted account data is not recoverable. Rotating replaces the account's key with another random one.

The built-in wallet is a read-only stand-in for the DApp Connector. Its seed comes from your passkey's PRF output, which needs a second prompt: first choose the passkey, then confirm to derive the seed. Without PRF it uses a random seed for this page only, so the wallet is empty and ephemeral. It never proves or pays; the service sponsors.
