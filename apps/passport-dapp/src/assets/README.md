Colours used by `src/styles.css`:

- **Midnight** (Brand Hub, midnight.network/brand-hub): Midnight Black `#0A0A0A`, White
  `#FFFFFF`, Blue `#0000FE` for accents only. The dark-theme surfaces, borders and the lighter blue
  (`#4F9CFF`) used where `#0000FE` lacks contrast on black come from the `midnight-did` docs-site
  theme (`docs-site/.vitepress/theme/custom.css`). Typeface: Outfit.
- **Lace** (lace.io and the logo's radial gradient): pink `#FF92DE` to yellow `#FDC300`, used only
  in the "Powered by Lace" badge.
